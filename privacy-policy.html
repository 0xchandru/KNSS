Privacy Policy

Keerthi Networks and Security Solution ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used.

Last updated: September 2026

01Information We Collect

This website is a static website. We do not operate a database or server-side storage of your information. When you use our enquiry, quotation, site visit or contact forms, the details you enter (such as your name, phone number, email, location and requirement) are formatted into a message and opened in WhatsApp (or your email client) for you to review and send to us. We receive the information only when you choose to send it.

02How We Use Your Information

Information you share with us is used solely to respond to your enquiry — to contact you, understand your requirement, prepare quotations, schedule site visits and provide the services you request. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

03WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

Enquiries submitted through this website are transmitted via WhatsApp, operated by a third party. Once you press Send within WhatsApp, your message is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy practices. We encourage you to review those separately. This website does not set advertising cookies, and no analytics identifiers are active unless explicitly enabled by us in future with appropriate disclosures.

04Communication Records

When you contact us, we may retain your message and related details on our own devices and business records for the purpose of servicing your enquiry and maintaining our customer relationship. You may ask us to delete your details at any time by writing to knsschennai@gmail.com.

05Your Choices

You are never required to share more information than you are comfortable with. Required form fields are limited to what we need to respond meaningfully. You may opt out of further communication from us at any time by telling us over WhatsApp, phone or email.

06Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website and services evolve. The latest version will always be published on this page.

07Contact

For any privacy-related questions, write to knsschennai@gmail.com or write to us at: Keerthi Networks and Security Solution, No. 159, Karuppasamy Koil Street, Dharmathupatti, Melachokkanathapuram, Bodinayakanur, Theni – 625582, Tamil Nadu, India.
