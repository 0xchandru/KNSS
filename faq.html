FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most. If your question is not answered here, message us on WhatsApp — we reply with straight, practical answers.

General

We are headquartered in Chennai and serve customers all over Tamil Nadu including Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, Tirunelveli, Erode, Theni and all districts across the state. Contact us to schedule a site visit or discuss your project.

We recommend a site assessment before quoting so the system is designed around your actual premises. Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or the site visit form to discuss and schedule a visit.

The fastest way is to message us on WhatsApp with your requirement — you can also use our Request a Quote form, which prepares a structured message for you. For accurate pricing we may suggest a quick site visit.

Both. We serve homes and individual customers as well as offices, shops, factories, warehouses, schools, hospitals, hotels, apartments and other organisations.

CCTV Surveillance

Yes. We design and install CCTV systems for homes, shops, offices, factories, warehouses and institutions — including camera supply, cabling, DVR/NVR setup, remote mobile viewing and after-sales support.

Yes. We configure secure remote viewing so you can see live and recorded footage on your smartphone from anywhere with internet access.

Yes, we take up maintenance and troubleshooting of existing systems — camera faults, DVR/NVR problems, cabling issues and upgrades.

Intercom & Video Door Phone

Yes. We install audio intercoms, video door phones and multi-unit intercom systems for homes, apartments, offices, hotels and commercial buildings.

Many intercom systems support electric lock or gate release from the indoor station. We check compatibility with your door and gate hardware during the survey.

Networking

Yes. Office LAN setup, CAT6/CAT6A structured cabling, network racks, patch panels, switches, routers, Wi-Fi coverage and network troubleshooting are core services for us.

Yes. We assess the building, plan access point positions and install a properly wired network so coverage is even and speeds are consistent across floors.

Biometric & Access Control

Yes. We supply and install fingerprint and face-recognition attendance systems with reporting software for offices, factories, schools and other organisations.

Yes. Access-control devices can drive electric locks, so the same fingerprint, face or card that marks attendance can also allow or deny entry through controlled doors.

Fire Safety

Yes. We supply ABC dry chemical powder, CO2, foam and other extinguisher types, along with fire buckets and safety accessories, for businesses and institutions.

Yes, we support inspection, refilling and maintenance of extinguishers, with records maintained. Contact us with your extinguisher details and quantities.
